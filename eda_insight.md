# Exploratory Data Analytics Insights

the following observations were identified during exploratory analysis of the dataset:

1. Placement rates increase significantly for students with a CPGA above 8.0.
2. DSA problem-solving ability is strongly associated with salary outcomes
3. Intership experiance has a measurable impact on placement probability.
4. Github activity demonstrates a positive relationship with compensation.
5. Open source contributors tend to exhibit stronger technical profiles.
6. Tier-1 institutions consistently report the highest average salaries.
7. CSE, AIML, and IT branches outperform other disciplines on placement metrics.
8. Communication skills contribute significantly to employability.
9. Students with multiple projects generally recieve better offers.
10. Competitive programming rating are positively correlated with placement .
11. Leadership experience contributes positively correlated outcome.
